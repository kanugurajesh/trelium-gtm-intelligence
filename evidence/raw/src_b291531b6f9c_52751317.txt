 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 

 What is branded swag and why is it important? 
 Branded swag consists of promotional merchandise customized with your company’s logo or message. This powerful tool enhances brand visibility, builds enduring customer loyalty, and creates memorable experiences. As a seasoned and dependable branded swag company, Stran helps your brand stand out and resonate with your audience. 

 What services does Stran offer besides swag selection? 
 Stran delivers an extensive range of printing and promotional solutions, crafted to complement your marketing strategy. From innovative design and robust e-commerce platforms to comprehensive fulfillment services and custom packaging, we're dedicated to bolstering your brand's presence and maximizing your return on investment. 

 How can Stran help with trade shows and events? 
 As a leading branded merchandise company, Stran orchestrates every detail of your event presence from start to finish. We manage pre-show mailings, attention-grabbing tradeshow giveaways, and seamless event execution. Our logistics solutions span North America, backed by extensive warehousing and fulfillment capabilities, making us the go-to corporate swag vendors dedicated to making your event a success. 

 What technology does Stran use to manage products? 
 At Stran, we harness the power of a Magento-based technology platform to oversee a diverse range of promotional products, gifts, and branded merchandise. This versatile platform is designed to integrate smoothly with existing software systems, enhancing efficiency and adaptability to meet all your promotional needs. We have all you expect from a leading promotional product supplier. 

 How can Stran create a custom loyalty program? 
 We craft personalized loyalty programs tailored to keep your customers engaged and returning. By leveraging data-driven insights, rewards, and branded merchandise, we’ll help you create programs that genuinely resonate with your clientele. 

 What is Stran's approach to marketing and branding? 
 As expert promotional merchandise distributors, we blend creativity, innovation, and sharp market insights to develop marketing strategies that connect with your target audience and reinforce your brand identity. 

 When was Stran founded and what is your expertise? 
 Established in 1994, Stran has over two decades of expertise in promotional marketing, branding, and logistics management. We serve a wide range of industries, delivering our services with unmatched professionalism and commitment to excellence.
