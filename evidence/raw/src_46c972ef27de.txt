 MISSION 
 ABOUT STRAN 
 Driving brand awareness and changing behavior through visual, creative, and technology solutions. 
 Our approach aims not only to drive awareness but also to foster a transformative impact on how individuals perceive and engage with a brand. Through the seamless integration of innovative visual elements, imaginative creativity, and state-of-the-art technology, we strive to create an immersive and memorable brand experience that goes beyond the conventional boundaries of marketing and initiating positive change in consumer behavior.

 Supporting Women to Shape the Future of Stran  
 Women On the Rise exists to give opportunities for women within Stran for career development, to see room for growth, and to be a part of shaping the future of Stran. 

 Chaired by our SVP of Client Strategy and VP of Sales, Women On the Rise hosts networking events and monthly meetings to  encourage: 
 Career development and growth 
 Networking across the company, industry and with business leaders across many industries 
 Educational and inspirational guest speakers 
 Opportunities to develop and showcase leadership skills 
 Offering both in-person and virtual experiences 
 Participation and representation at industry events
