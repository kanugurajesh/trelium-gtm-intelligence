 About Us

 Our Story... 
 High Caliber Line (HCL) is a family-owned and operated business that has been a trusted name in the promotional products industry since 1999. Founded under Calibre International, HCL stands out for its innovation, exceptional customer service, and competitive pricing, working with a wide network of distributors across North America. What sets us apart is our commitment to our roots—our founder remains actively involved, fostering a strong family culture that runs throughout the entire team. 
 From our humble beginnings specializing in lapel pins, we've grown into an award-winning company with over 25 years of experience. Today, we operate three state-of-the-art facilities in the Greater Los Angeles area, including a 100,000 sq. ft. production and office space in Southern California, where we produce approximately 30 million items annually. Our end-to-end, in-house process—covering production, fulfillment, and distribution—ensures the highest quality standards and quicker lead times for our clients. 
 Our extensive product range includes premium stainless-steel drinkware, bamboo and acacia cutting boards, UltraHyde notebooks, trendy nylon and polyester bags, and high-capacity power banks. We also take pride in our Eco-Friendly collection, offering items made from sustainable or recycled materials such as reusable bags, bamboo notebooks, pens, and wheat straw drinkware. 
 With a diverse array of imprint and engraving options, we offer detailed and bold branding on every product. Whether through full-color digital printing, dye sublimation, pad printing, or debossing, our custom department tailors each project to meet specific client needs. Trusted by major brands and Fortune 500 companies, our decades of experience reflect the consistent demand for our quality products and services. 
 At HCL, every department—from executive management to purchasing, customer service, IT, production, and shipping—works together to ensure each project is delivered to perfection and on time, providing a truly ‘High Caliber’ experience for our clients and their customers.
