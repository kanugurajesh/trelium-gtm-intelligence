 Titleist Custom Outing Program 
 May 14, 2026 Kristin Lamusga 

 Program Overview 
 The Titleist Custom Outing Program is available for qualifying accounts. It supports custom golf ball and gear orders for tournaments, outings, corporate events, and country club programs.  
 Program Discount: off wholesale pricing 
 Available For: Titleist golf balls and Titleist gear 
 Eligible Products Include: 
 All Titleist golf balls 
 Titleist golf gear 
 Custom packaging options 
 Customization Requirements 
 Golf balls must include personalization tied to the event, such as: 
 Tournament name 
 Outing name 
 Event date 
 Logo with event name or date 
 Corporate logo-only golf ball orders require tournament or outing verification.
