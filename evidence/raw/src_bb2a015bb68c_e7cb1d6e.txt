 Electronic Integrations 

 Free up valuable hours in the day and save your business time and money by connecting your systems to ours. 

 WHY INTEGRATE? 
 A SanMar systems integration keeps your people focused on what they’re good at, instead of tracking down where that last order went. They’ll be able to do business seamlessly without having to pick up the phone or send an email. Benefits include: 
 Product inventory availability: Real-time 
 Order entry: Simple and familiar 
 Invoices: The way you want them 
 Information: Easy to find and available 24/7 
 We’ve created a chart to help you understand the benefits of integration, as well as the different types of integration we offer. 
 Download it here. 

 INTEGRATION MODELS AVAILABLE 
 Your business is unique, so we offer several ways to connect with you. 
 The PromoStandards integration platform is a shared industry resource, connecting you to SanMar’s up-to-the-minute product data as well as many other promotional product organizations. Learn more at https://promostandards.org 
 The SanMar flat file platform provides you with current product information to load into your system on demand. 
 The SanMar integration platform is a direct and up-to-the-minute connection between your systems and SanMar’s data, including product information as well as invoicing and shipping data. 

 WE’RE HERE TO HELP 
 Our team of integration experts will work with your IT staff to implement the best option for your business. Contact us and put our technology to work for your business.
