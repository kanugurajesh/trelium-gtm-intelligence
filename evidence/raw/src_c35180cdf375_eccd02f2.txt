 What We Do Employee Recognition & Incentives 
 Employee Recognition Platforms

 Consolidate all of your appreciation & incentive initiatives with a employee achievement recognition program. Request a Demo 

 Engage employees wherever they are located with our powerful SaaS-based employee recognition solutions . 

 Aligned with your core values and business goals, HALO’s employee recognition solutions build and support a culture that encourages and recognizes outstanding performance. 

 Unlimited Initiatives 
 Create and customize an unlimited number of initiatives to build an employee experience that supports your goals and culture. 

 Accessible Anywhere 
 Cloud-based HR software provides a consistent experience for worldwide, remote, and offline workforces. 

 World-Class Support 
 Personalized account teams, marketing campaigns, and highly rated customer service for participants along with ongoing strategic consultation. 

 Strategic design 

 Our top-down approach to program design is essential to long-term success. A recognition program must resonate with and engage your workforce to achieve your organization’s recognition goals. The design phase begins with high-level strategic sessions to help us fully understand your employee achievement recognition goals. We collaborate with clients to ensure project direction aligns with business goals and objectives, maximizing success and return on investment. 

 Make employee recognition a part of your culture

 Get in touch 

 Rewards for everyone 

 A universal, points-based rewards currency puts the largest selection of employee achievement awards and the power of choice at your employees’ fingertips. 

 Gift cards from major retailers — great for global or remote teams. 
 Vacation bundles, concert tickets, and other experiential employee rewards. 
 Donations to charitable organizations for employees who want to give back. 
 One of the largest selection of employee achievement gifts anywhere, with products in every category. 

 Social recognition 

 Being social and sharing recognition far and wide is essential to companywide culture building. HALO’s cloud-based employee recognition software allows for deep personalization, dynamic program updates, and the ability to connect employees across departments and regions during celebrations with easy-to-use tools. 

 A world of recognition 

 HALO offers streamlined solutions for cost-efficient worldwide programs, all from a single employee recognition platform. 

 Global Reward Selection 
 Recognize, reward, and inspire employees consistently no matter where they are in the world. 

 In-Country Fulfillment 
 Save on international shipping costs with convenient in-country fulfillment of rewards. 

 Culturally Appropriate 
 Regional gift catalogs based on local tastes and customs with multilingual customer service and translation services.
